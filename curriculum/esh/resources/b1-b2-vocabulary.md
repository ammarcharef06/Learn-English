## Usage checks

- `Evidence`, `research`, and `feedback` are generally uncountable in these meanings: `some evidence`, `a piece of evidence`, `feedback`, not ordinarily `an evidence` or `feedbacks`.
- Say `raise a concern`, `run a trial`, `reach an agreement`, and `allocate funds`; these are useful collocations, not fixed translation equivalents.
- `Reliable` describes trustworthiness in context; `valid` usually means well-founded or acceptable for a stated purpose. Do not treat them as interchangeable.
- `Associated with` indicates a relationship; it does not by itself mean `caused`.
- Regional spelling and lexical choices may vary. Record the variety used in an assessment; accept standard alternatives that preserve meaning.
