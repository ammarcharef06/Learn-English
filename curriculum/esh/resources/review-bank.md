# Review and spaced retrieval bank

## Review principles

- revisit items after 24 hours, 3 days, 7 days, and 14 days
- reintroduce grammar in new contexts
- combine recall with sentence production
- keep assessment brief and specific

## Sample cards

### Card 1
- Target: `I am late.`
- Prompt: Say the sentence in a negative form.
- Answer: `I am not late.`

### Card 2
- Target: simple past of `go`
- Prompt: Complete the sentence: `Last weekend, I ___ to the market.`
- Answer: `went`

### Card 3
- Target: `should`
- Prompt: Give advice to a tired student.
- Answer: `She should rest and sleep.`

### Card 4
- Target: `however`
- Prompt: Write a sentence that contrasts two ideas.
- Answer: `The plan is practical; however, it is expensive.`

## Use note

Review should be frequent but purposeful. It is better to revisit a small number of high-value items with varied contexts than to mass-produce repetitive flashcards.
