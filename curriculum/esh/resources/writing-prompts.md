# Writing prompts

## A1 prompts

- Write 6 sentences about your daily routine.
- Write a short paragraph about your family.
- Describe your home and the rooms you use most.

## A2 prompts

- Describe a trip you took last year.
- Write about a typical weekend.
- Explain how you plan to spend your next holiday.

## B1 prompts

- Write a paragraph about a local problem and a practical solution.
- Describe a good or bad experience at work or school.
- Explain whether online learning is better than classroom learning.

## B2 prompts

- Write an essay on the advantages and disadvantages of remote work.
- Discuss whether public transport should be improved in your city.
- Explain how technology affects social relationships.

## C1 prompts

- Write a formal report comparing two possible policies.
- Summarize and evaluate a research article in 400–500 words.
- Draft a professional email responding to a client concern.

## C2 prompts

- Write a persuasive speech about a major public issue.
- Prepare a briefing that explains risk, evidence, and recommendation.
- Draft a formal review of a policy or event with nuanced analysis.

## Post-C2 prompts

- Write a research abstract for a professional or academic topic.
- Prepare a policy memo for a leadership team.
- Produce an editorial revision of a dense passage for clarity and tone.
