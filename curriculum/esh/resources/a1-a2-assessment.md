# A1–A2 diagnostic, unit checks, and stage tests

**Scope:** local, criterion-referenced course checks for A1 and A2 only. The point thresholds below are instructional decisions, not norm-referenced scores, CEFR certification, or proof of level attainment. They require a teacher/partner for spoken performance. Listening scripts remain available for live teacher reading; selected indexed prompts have supplemental experimental Amy recordings. No automated pronunciation/listening score is supplied, and generated files are not validated assessment evidence.

## How to record performance

Record each task as **attempted**, **completed**, or **mastered**. Opening or reading a lesson is not evidence of mastery. A controlled item is mastered only when the keyed response is correct. Open speaking/writing tasks use the specific criteria stated for that task. Record support given and repeat missed targets with new, parallel items after a delay.

Each unit check is **8 points**: listening (1), reading (1), language (2), interaction (2), and writing (2). For listening and reading, one point requires both keyed details. For language, each of two items is one point. Interaction is one point per criterion. Writing is one point per criterion. The check is passed at **6/8**, provided the learner earns at least one point in interaction and one in writing; otherwise repair that skill before retesting. The key permits reasonable alternatives.

## Remediation and retesting protocol

1. Mark the exact item or rubric criterion missed; do not label a whole skill from one item.
2. Teach/rehearse the related lesson with a fresh example and reduced support.
3. After a delay, administer a parallel item with changed names, details, or prompts.
4. Record original attempt, support, retest result, and whether performance transferred to an unfamiliar prompt.
5. Listening comprehension should be checked without showing the script first. Oral intelligibility and interaction criteria require a trained human listener; this document does not provide recorded audio or automated speech scoring.
