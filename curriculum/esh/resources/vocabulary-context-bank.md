## Selected collocations and contrasts

- `make a decision` / `reach a decision`; not usually `do a decision`.
- `give advice` / `a piece of advice`; `advice` is uncountable in standard English.
- `strong evidence` / `limited evidence`; `evidence` is normally uncountable.
- `do research` / `conduct research`; both are standard, with register variation.
- `reduce costs` / `reduce delays`; `reduce` takes a direct object in these examples.
- `reliable service` / `reliable information`; meaning depends on the noun.
- `preserve quality`; `compromise quality` can mean weaken it; `without compromising quality` means while maintaining it.
